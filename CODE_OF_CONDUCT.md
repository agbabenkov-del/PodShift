# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

PodShift welcomes constructive technical discussion and contributions from people with different levels of experience.

Be respectful, stay focused on the project, and criticize ideas or code rather than people. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, threats, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope

This applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may restrict participation when behavior repeatedly harms the project or its contributors.

When in doubt, assume good faith and keep the conversation useful. There are already enough fights available in the world without adding one over a Bluetooth utility.
